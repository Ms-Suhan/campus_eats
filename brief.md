CampusEats Brief

What the system does

CampusEats is a campus food ordering system that allows students to discover food available on campus, select items from menus, place orders, and receive updates about their orders. The system connects students with food vendors and manages the complete ordering process from selecting food to receiving the order.

The main goal of CampusEats is to make campus food ordering easier, faster, and more organized. Instead of students having to visit a food vendor and wait in a queue, they can use the system to view available food, create an order, and track its progress.

Who uses it

* Student/Customer — browses menus, selects food, places orders, makes payments, and checks order status.
* Food Vendor/Restaurant — manages its menu, receives orders, accepts or rejects orders, and updates order status.
* Delivery Staff — handles food delivery and updates delivery information.
* Administrator — manages users, vendors, and the overall CampusEats system.

Summary

CampusEats brings students, food vendors, delivery staff, and administrators together in one system. Students use it to order food conveniently, vendors use it to manage menus and orders, delivery staff use it to handle deliveries, and administrators manage the platform. The main flow is to browse food, add items to a cart, place and pay for an order, prepare it, deliver it, and update the student throughout the process.
